## Address notes (data.js compared with the official site)
- vent: the official address adds "フェスタ表参道ビルB1", which data.js leaves out. The official Maps pin is named "Wall＆Wall（VENT Tokyo）". data.js hours 23:00~05:00 are not stated on the official site.
- mitsuki: the official site publishes no street address, only "top of Dogenzaka" plus a Maps pin, so data.js 1-22-12 Dogenzaka can't be checked. The official name is 翠月 -MITSUKI-.
- saloon: data.js has area=SHIBUYA, but it's in the same building as UNIT (area=DAIKANYAMA), and the official access info is Daikanyama Sta. 1 min.
- compufunk: the address matches a 2024 listing. The official page's Japanese text is garbled, so I couldn't confirm it there.
- All other addresses agree with the official sites.
