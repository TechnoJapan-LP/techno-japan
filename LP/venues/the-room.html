THE ROOM

HOUSEOTHERSLIVE

This intimate Shibuya basement bar operates as a low-key refuge for serious music heads, programming everything from deep house to experimental electronics. The space maintains a deliberately understated atmosphere, drawing a knowing crowd that values sound quality over spectacle. Its compact layout creates an almost living room-like intimacy between DJs and dancers.
