SAWAGI FESTIVALOne of Japan's defining local festivals passes its ten-year mark and moves from Yamanashi to Gunma.

WORDS BY
TECHNO JAPAN Editorial
PUBLISHED
OCT 19, 2026
READING TIME
2 MIN
Having passed the ten-year milestone last year, this camp-in outdoor festival now enters its eleventh edition. For 2026 it moves from Yamanashi to SUTEKI VILLAGE CAMPING in Higashiagatsuma, Gunma. Two stages, six live acts, 52 DJs. DJs who have built scenes in Sapporo, Osaka and Kitakyushu gather in the mountains of Gunma.

Sapporo, Osaka, Kitakyushu. DJs who have built scenes in their own cities converge on a mountain in Gunma.

WHAT IS SAWAGI FESTIVAL

A camp-in open-air festival. Held in Yamanashi for years, it moves to Gunma in 2026, its eleventh edition. The organisers' banner phrase: "deepening and expansion."

Fifty-eight acts across two stages, with DECO / ART and VJ / LIGHTING teams shaping the site. Tickets are three-day passes only. The tent site is free, and the whole thing is built around staying on site for the duration.

Cradled by mountains, facing a sunrise off the horizon

The venue is SUTEKI VILLAGE CAMPING in Higashiagatsuma, Agatsuma District, Gunma. Roughly 33,000 square metres of mountain grounds, with auto sites, free sites and sectioned plots spread across them. A sky full of stars at night; the sun climbing off the horizon in the morning.

On site there are showers, toilets, washrooms and a kitchen. For the festival, the flat grass tent site is free to use, and auto-camping plots of around 100㎡ are also available. A shuttle bus runs from Shibuya.

100 keyboards, and psychedelic rock formed in 1993

Six live acts. ASUNA + Opitope pairs ASUNA — whose performance piece "100 Keyboards," using one hundred keyboards, has taken him to festivals abroad and to BAM in New York — with Opitope, formed in 2002 by Tomoyoshi Date and Chihei Hatakeyama. SOFT, formed in 1993, is a band working in the lineage of psychedelic rock. Black Boboi, HENTAI CAMERAMAN, Masafumi Take & Sabatoj and SUEPON follow.

Names that have held up Japan's club scene for decades

Fifty-two DJs. DJ KENSEI, DJ KURI, DJ SODEYAMA, DJ ZEN, COMPUMA — the latter also known as a buyer at Osaka's NEWTONE RECORDS.

TAKAAKI ITOH, who runs the label WOLS; OCCA, who has kept "Archiv" going at Sapporo's Precious Hall; YSK, who runs the Kitakyushu venue α and has played Berghain. Also Mari Sakurai, REE.K, YAMA, Herbalistek, Riyo Sanmyaku and more. Sapporo, Osaka, Kitakyushu. DJs who have built scenes in their own cities converge on a mountain in Gunma.

Dressing a mountain campsite in decor and light

DECO / ART comes from six crews: Bivouac, Bubble Art Onchi, HEAVEN HUG DESIGN, R領域, SAMAYA DESIGN and STONE63. VJ / LIGHTING is handled by four: comboy, DOT TV, idealsolution and sarunotuku.

Event details

  • Dates: 2026.10.23 (Fri) 13:00 GATE OPEN – 10.25 (Sun) 18:00 CLOSE
  • Venue: SUTEKI VILLAGE CAMPING (3330-5 Motojuku, Higashiagatsuma, Agatsuma District, Gunma)
  • Tickets: U25 ¥10,000 / Category 3 ¥16,000 / Door ¥20,000. Three-day passes only (ZAIKO)
  • Tent site: free (flat grass)
  • Auto camping: ¥30,000 per plot (approx. 100㎡). Reserve via the form on the official site
  • On-site parking: free in/out ¥10,000 (exit and re-entry only between 8:00 and 17:00) / fixed parking ¥3,000 (no re-entry)
  • Shuttle bus: departs from in front of Cerulean Tower, Shibuya. Round trip ¥10,000 / one way ¥7,000. Reserve via the form on the official site
  • Official: sawagifestival.com / @sawagifestival_jp
