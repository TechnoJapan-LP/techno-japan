GROW THE CULTURE OPEN AIR

The fourth outdoor chapter from GROW THE CULTURE, the label and collective launched in Tokyo in 2021. Billed as "Tokyo's only bass music festival," it builds on UK sound system culture with 20 hours of genre-crossing low end echoing through Ohba Camping Village in Hinode. Sound comes courtesy of a VOID Acoustics rig tuned specifically for GTC. This year welcomes Fracture and Neffa-T from the UK, alongside 17 acts including akii, Dayzero, Dx, okadada, Stones Taro, Submerse and Velocity. Bungalows and campsites let you stay through until morning. Nights get cold — hand warmers are handed out at the entrance.

SANGENJAYA ART MUSIC FESTIVAL

Under the banner "the streets of Sangenjaya connected by music," this urban festival turns the neighbourhood itself into a circuit, running from midday through to the next morning. Daytime centres on the NTT East Setagaya Building parking lot, with live sets, DJs, food and a market spread across four official venues; from 18:00 it shifts into "OPEN TOWN TIME," where you roam the area's bars and restaurants at will. After 20:00, four night venues including FRIENDS bar&lounge and Tengu Shokudo carry hip hop, house, disco, soul, jazz and funk through to dawn. Around 70 acts, among them JESSE, DABO, Yamaarashi, RUEED, BARK and OSAMU M. Admission is free, and ordering the official drink "Sancha HIGH" earns you a sticker at each venue.

PROX

A music and camping festival making its debut at DAN OISO in Oiso, Kanagawa. The tagline: "Live by day, rave by night." The day programme, from 15:00 to 23:00, features live sets from HUGEN, Liberal a.k.a Toshiki Iwama, Fried Banana Shop and Yume (BOY), while the night, 23:00 to 5:00, is handled by SUNGAHOUSE, Dazzle Drums, Yuki Kawamura and Hiroki Uzawa. Roughly an hour from Shibuya or Shinjuku on the Shonan-Shinjuku Line. Designed so you can come for the day or pitch a tent and stay until morning: a full pass is ¥8,000, plus ¥3,000 per tent for camping. Artwork by Sota Obara and Yo Suzaki.

MUTEK.JP

The Japanese edition of the Montreal-born international festival of electronic music and digital creativity reaches its 11th year. Co-presented with JR East, it moves this year to MoN Takanawa in Takanawa Gateway City under the theme "New Cycle!" Highlights include a 3D audiovisual live set from Max Cooper built around his new album and the Japan premiere of NEUROSCAN by Robert Lippok and Lucas Gutierrez, with more than 20 acts from Japan and abroad including Ryoichi Kurokawa, Daito Manabe × ELEVENPLAY and Hania Rani. Four days combining standing and seated formats, with conferences during the day and the concurrent exhibition "ETERNAL Art Space." A four-day passport is ¥25,000.

SOUL BEAT ASIA

The "Under the Bridge World Music Festival," staged once again at Sengoku Park, sprawling beneath Toyota Ohashi Bridge in Toyota, Aichi. Tickets are called "entry permits to the autonomous zone under the bridge," framed as a contribution toward taking part in the festival. Built by hand together with volunteers, this year's first wave of announcements lists 17 acts, among them TURTLE ISLAND, OKI DUB AINU BAND, Yuta Orisaka Band, DEATH SIDE, SAICOBAB, T-Jiro's, neco neru, Oni no Migiude, Korean nongak troupe Noripan, Awa Odori group Taiko-ren and min'yo ensemble Hosenkai. As the site sits in a residential area, taiko and high-volume performances are kept to a minimum after 21:00. Advance tickets are ¥3,000 for one day, ¥7,500 for all three. Free at the door for junior high students and under, and for those 65 and over.

Resonance

An event hosted by DJ and artist Lisa Mizuno where electronic music, art and wellness intersect. The name refers to the process by which stimuli from outside resonate within us and give rise to new sensations. After five editions in Tokyo, it moved last year to Oizumi Factory in Kawaguchi, Saitama. The roughly 10,000㎡ site holds two floors: the industrial indoor WAREHOUSE and the GARDEN, where sound dissolves into its surroundings. Melbourne's LOIF, Berlin-based PERYL, "African Acid is the Future" head Maryama Luiccioni, Fried Banana Shop and SAKURA TSURUTA line up, alongside workshops in yoga, tea ceremony, meditation and running. Attendees are asked to bring their own cups and tableware.

Cabaret Recordings

The party from Cabaret Recordings — the label founded by So Inagawa and dj masda in 2013 — heads to Miyakojima. The venue is Mipama Es Su Casa, facing Yonaha Maehama Beach. The sound of a label built on a catalogue rooted in minimal rings out for one night on a Miyakojima beach in late November.
