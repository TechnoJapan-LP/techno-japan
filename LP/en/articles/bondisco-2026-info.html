26 Hours in the Woods of Sanda, Hyogo: DIY Party BON DISCO Heads Into Its 13th Summer

Set deep in the woods of Sanda, Hyōgo, the DIY party BON DISCO — where dancing runs from noon straight through to the following afternoon — is heading into its 13th summer. Nearly 40 acts from across Japan, two floors, and a pool to cool your body down before diving back into the sound: this is a way of spending time that only midsummer can offer.

A DIY party in the forests of Sanda, Hyogo — dancing straight through a summer weekend

It kicks off at noon on August 22 and runs until the afternoon of the 23rd. "BON DISCO," an independent DIY party built entirely by its own crew, greets its 13th summer at Sanda Athletic Park in Sanda, Hyogo.

What is BON DISCO

An independent DIY party built with their own hands. Surrounded by abundant nature, it aims to create a space where anyone can break free from the everyday as fully as possible — and in 2026 it reaches its 13th year.

No leaning on big capital, no leaning on trends. The relationships BON DISCO has built up over 13 years are, quite literally, the shape this party takes.

A setting of forest and pools, far from the city's heatwave

The venue is Sanda Athletic Park in Sanda, Hyogo — the second year running after last year. The tree-lined environment itself shapes the experience of this party.

One of its charms is the pool area. There are two pools — a regular one for a leisurely cool-down, and a shallow one where even small children can play safely — plus full shower facilities. This year there will be no sauna and no poolside DJs; instead it opens as a pure relaxation space where you can quietly take in the breath of the forest.

Cool your danced-out body in the water, then head back to the sound. That round trip is how you spend BON DISCO in the height of summer.

Close to 40 acts across two meticulously crafted floors.

Two floors built around a commitment to high-quality sound guide you toward a deeper, more immersive musical experience. The lineup features close to 40 artists, centered on Kansai but gathering from across the country.

Beyond the floors, Naoko hosts yoga sessions, while 電子原人, 佐々木ビル (Yabar食堂 & 太陽の村) and Vamos Tacos line up the food. Music, rest and eating all run seamlessly together within the same forest.

INFORMATION

Admission is ¥7,000. Free for those aged 18 and under.

The entrance fee is the only cost — there are no charges whatsoever for parking, tents or pool use. Only those wishing to stay in the paid lodges need to book directly with Sanda Athletic in advance.

Dance, cool off, dance again.
At the end of that cycle lies the 13th summer.
