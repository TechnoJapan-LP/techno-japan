Resonance24 hours crossing electronic music, wellness and art at Oizumi Kojo, Kawaguchi

Resonance runs for 24 hours from 10am on 22 November at Oizumi Kojo in Kawaguchi, Saitama, with DJs and live acts across a warehouse and a garden, plus yoga, tea ceremony and meditation.

From 10:00 on Sunday, November 22 to 10:00 on Monday, November 23 (a national holiday), Resonance takes over OKS KAWAGUCHI CAMPUS, the grounds of Oizumi Factory in Kawaguchi, Saitama. A 24-hour event spanning electronic music, wellness and art, this marks its second edition at the venue.

Two stages—WAREHOUSE, set in a former storehouse, and GARDEN, out in the grounds—host DJs and live acts from Japan and abroad. A traditional Japanese house will stage tea ceremony and meditation sessions, while yoga takes place in the GARDEN.

What is Resonance?

Resonance is a project led by DJ Lisa Mizuno. The name comes from the Latin resonare (to sound back), and the project takes as its subject the very process by which stimuli received from outside echo within us and move toward harmony. Cutting across club culture, installation art and neuroscience, it treats electronic music as "an experience of perception itself."

After repeated editions at Tokyo venues including ENTER, Forestlimit and Kaguwa, it has invited artists from Berlin, Prague and Korea. In April 2025 it relocated to Oizumi Factory, drawing a cumulative audience of 400.

Organiser Lisa Mizuno offered the following comment on this edition:

Rather than simply "listening" to sound, you feel it with your body, become one with the space, and touch something that lies within you. Through that experience you recover a "sensory harmony" and gradually return to yourself as you are. I hope to share that kind of time with everyone who comes.

The venue: Oizumi Factory, founded over a century ago

OKS KAWAGUCHI CAMPUS is the headquarters site of Oizumi Factory Co., Ltd., founded in 1917. Set within Kawaguchi's industrial district, the roughly 3,000-tsubo plot is unusually green. A storehouse once used for manufacturing, a garden, a Western-style building registered as a tangible cultural property and a traditional Japanese house all stand on the same grounds.

The site also houses an organic kombucha brewery and taproom, a plant-based café and a micro farm. Oizumi Factory co-presents Resonance, serving as a co-creation partner for the second year running.

WAREHOUSE: the night floor

The storehouse floor opens at 19:00 and runs through to 5:00. Things begin with a live set from Alminium and ZUNDOKODISCO, with Melbourne's LOIF from 22:00. Known for Plunge on 〈Animalia〉 and the album GHOSTWORLD, LOIF builds a sound in which techno, psy, bass and breaks blend and flow into one another.

From midnight, organiser Lisa Mizuno performs alongside movement artist Shoichi Tanaka. At 1:30am, Berlin's PERYL plays live, working from dub techno textures and low end, minimal repetition and reverberation.

From 3:00 to 5:00, Liquid Drop Groove's YUTA closes things out. Spatial production is handled by RGB.

GARDEN: from morning to the next morning

GARDEN is produced by Mural, responsible for numerous events across Tokyo, with architect and designer Sekikoh contributing the stage design. It opens at 10:00 with anym, moving into a midday live set from Sakura Tsuruta, who studied music therapy and electronic production design at Berklee College of Music. Maryisonacid, founder of the Berlin label and party 〈African Acid is the Future〉, plays next.

Kaishin Shimizu, masao and Mural's Kai follow, and from 20:00 three-piece jam band Fried Banana Shop take to the stage. Through the night the action shifts to WAREHOUSE, before GARDEN reopens at 5:00. Reo Anzai, a live set from Akoorum and ΛKΛRI carry it through to 10:00 on the 23rd.

Wellness and workshops

The other pillar alongside the music is a programme of workshops that engage with bodily sensation. Part of the "Sound Flow Studio" project, it returns for a second year. Yoga takes place three times: morning, evening and the following morning, unfolding on the GARDEN stage in combination with healing live sets, DJs and live performance.

In the Japanese house, there are two tea ceremony sittings by Tyaerbi Epoche with wagashi from HAODORI, plus two meditation sessions led by Shunsuke Sakami. The space is designed by Yuna Kwon, with ikebana by Itsubo Manato. From 16:30 there is also a talk session between Lisa Mizuno and Neutone.

A run hosted by running community LIKOU is free and requires no reservation. A tour of Oizumi Factory and a workshop for children are also planned.

Zero waste and venue rules

The event operates on a zero-waste policy, with no bins on site. Attendees are asked to take their rubbish home, and bringing your own cup and tableware is encouraged. Food vendors also plan to offer cup and tableware rentals. _SHIP KOMBUCHA, an organic fermented sparkling tea brewed on site, will be served.

For the sake of wellbeing and the preservation of the historic buildings, the entire site is non-smoking. Cans, bottles and alcohol may not be brought in. With a wristband, re-entry is free throughout the event. Preschool-age children enter free, and attendees are asked to refrain from arriving with them after 21:00.
