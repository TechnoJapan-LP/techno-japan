Festival Submission

Techno Japan lists techno and underground electronic music festivals
and events held in Japan. Listing is free of charge.
Please use the form below to submit your event.

About listings

Our editorial team decides which festivals and events to list,
based on the direction of the publication.

  • Listing is free of charge. No fees are involved
  • Submission does not guarantee publication
  • We are unable to respond individually regarding listing decisions
  • Submitted information may be edited by our editorial team
  • For corrections or removal requests after publication,
    please contact us via the address provided in the form

What we cover

We focus on festivals and events centered on electronic music,
including techno, house, minimal, ambient, and experimental.

How it works

  1. Submit via the form
  2. Our editorial team reviews the submission
  3. If accepted, the event is published on the site
    (For time-sensitive submissions, please note this in the remarks field)
Go to submission form

We also welcome updates after publication, including
additional lineup announcements. Please use the same form.
