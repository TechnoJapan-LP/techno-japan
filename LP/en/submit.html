01 — About listings

How it works

The editorial team reviews festival and event listings against the direction of the media. For our approach, see STATEMENT .

01Listing is free. There is no charge for publication.
02We cannot guarantee that every submitted event will be listed.
03We do not provide individual explanations for listing decisions.
04The editorial team may edit the information provided when publishing it.
05For corrections or removal after publication, please use the contact details in the form.
02 — What we list

Genres

We focus on festivals and events centered on electronic music, including techno, house, minimal, ambient and experimental music.
