Techno Japan lists techno and underground electronic music festivals and events held in Japan. Listing is free of charge. Please apply using the form below.

About listings

The editorial team reviews festival and event listings against the direction of the media. For our approach, see STATEMENT .

  • Listing is free. There is no charge for publication.
  • We cannot guarantee that every submitted event will be listed.
  • We do not provide individual explanations for listing decisions.
  • The editorial team may edit the information provided when publishing it.
  • For corrections or removal after publication, please use the contact details in the form.

What we list

We focus on festivals and events centered on electronic music, including techno, house, minimal, ambient and experimental music.

How it works

  1. Submit the form
  2. The editorial team reviews your information
  3. If approved, your event will be published on the site
    (If your request is urgent, please note that in the comments.)
