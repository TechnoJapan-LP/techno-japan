Rainbow Disco Club

Rainbow Disco Club is a techno / house festival held in Shizuoka. The latest listed edition is April 17, 2026 to April 19, 2026 at Higashi-Izu Cross Country Course.

TECHNOHOUSE

Rainbow Disco Clubは静岡県の東伊豆クロスカントリーコースで開催され、自然の地形をそのまま屋外のダンスフロアへと変えるフェスティバルだ。ハウスとテクノを軸としたプログラムに、日本ならではの美しい田園風景が組み合わさっているのが特徴。こぢんまりとした規模感と、海外勢と国内勢のバランスを丁寧に考え抜いたラインナップで知られている。

Rainbow Disco Club takes place at the Higashi-Izu Cross Country Course in Shizuoka, transforming the natural terrain into an outdoor dance floor. The festival combines house and techno programming with Japan's scenic countryside setting. Known for its intimate scale and carefully curated lineup that balances international and domestic acts.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

When is Rainbow Disco Club held?
The latest listed date for Rainbow Disco Club is April 17, 2026 to April 19, 2026.
Where is Rainbow Disco Club held?
Rainbow Disco Club is held at Higashi-Izu Cross Country Course, Inatori, Higashiizu-cho, Kamo-gun, Shizuoka Prefecture, Shizuoka.
Which artists are playing at Rainbow Disco Club?
The lineup includes Antal & Hunee, Ben Ufo, Daphni, Dj Maria., Dungeoneering (Albino Sound & Daigos), Feline, Floating Points, Gerd Janson, Gonno, Haai, Helena Hauff, Jonathan Kusuma, Jonny Rock, Kikiorix, Mala, NC4K (Stones Taro & Lomax), Sisi b2b Ouissam b2b Yamarchy, Suze Ijó, Four Tet.
