Sawagi Festival

Suteki Village Camping Ground, Gunma↗
OFFICIAL SITETICKETS

SAWAGI FESTIVALはアンダーグラウンド・シーンを牽引する国内屈指のDJ・アーティストが一堂に集結し、ダンスミュージックの真髄を極めた重厚なサウンドと熱量の高いパフォーマンスを展開。標高の高い豊かな自然に囲まれた開放的な空間で、音とスピリットを全身で共有する圧倒的な没入感と、自由でアットホームなコミュニティ感が魅力のオープンエア・パーティです。

SAWAGI FESTIVAL brings together top-tier domestic DJs and artists leading the underground scene, delivering high-energy performances with a heavy, immersive sound that captures the true essence of dance music. Set in an open, elevated natural landscape, this outdoor party offers a deeply immersive experience where music and spirit are shared freely within a welcoming, homey community atmosphere.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

When is Sawagi Festival held?

The latest listed date for Sawagi Festival is October 23, 2026 to October 25, 2026.

Where is Sawagi Festival held?

Sawagi Festival is held at Suteki Village Camping Ground, 群馬県吾妻郡東吾妻町本宿3330-5, Gunma.

Where can I buy tickets for Sawagi Festival?

Tickets for Sawagi Festival are available at https://sawagi-festival.zaiko.io/ja/e/sawagi-festival-2026.

Which artists are playing at Sawagi Festival?

Sawagi Festival 2026 lineup: Black Boboi, HENTAI CAMERAMAN, SOFT, ADAK7, Akira Arasawa, COMPUMA, DJ KENSEI, DJ KURI, DJ SODEYAMA, DJ ZEN and 22 more.
