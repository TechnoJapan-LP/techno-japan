THE STAR FESTIVAL

THE STAR FESTIVAL is a techno / house / bass festival held in Kyoto. The latest listed edition is May 16, 2026 to May 17, 2026 at Fuminno Mori Hiyoshi.

TECHNOHOUSEBASS

THE STAR FESTIVALは、京都という歴史ある土地に日本のアンダーグラウンド・エレクトロニック・ミュージック・コミュニティを結集させるフェスティバルだ。テクノとハウスを軸に、名の知れたアーティストから新進気鋭のアクトまでを紹介し、伝統と革新的なカルチャーが融合するこの街ならではの空気感を映し出す、親密な集いを作り出している。丁寧に練り上げられたラインナップと、雰囲気のあるヴェニュー選びで知られている。

THE STAR FESTIVAL brings together Japan's underground electronic music community in Kyoto's historic setting. The festival showcases both established and emerging artists across techno and house, creating an intimate gathering that reflects the city's unique blend of tradition and forward-thinking culture. Known for its carefully curated lineups and atmospheric venues.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

When is THE STAR FESTIVAL held?
The latest listed date for THE STAR FESTIVAL is May 16, 2026 to May 17, 2026.
Where is THE STAR FESTIVAL held?
THE STAR FESTIVAL is held at Fuminno Mori Hiyoshi, 25 Tenwaka Kaminosho, Hiyoshi-cho, Nantan City, Kyoto Prefecture, Kyoto.
Where can I buy tickets for THE STAR FESTIVAL?
Tickets for THE STAR FESTIVAL are available at https://thestarfestival.zaiko.io/e/2026kyoto.
Which artists are playing at THE STAR FESTIVAL?
The lineup includes CLIPZ, DJ HYPE, dj masda, Guchon, Lady Shaka, MAKOTO, MC GQ, Minna-no-kimochi, nasthug, okadada, Powder, ralph, riria, ryota, SHERELLE.
