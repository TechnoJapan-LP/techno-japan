SEP 5 — 6, 2026

FOREST SOUND CAMP

Ooba Camp Village — Tokyo↗

東京のクラブシーンでイベントを企画してきたUniversal Music Box/UMBが主催する、キャンプ型のオールナイトレイヴ。幼い頃から森に親しみ、林学を学んできたメンバーが、「森林と音楽の共存」をコンセプトに立ち上げた。舞台は、地域の約8割を森林が占める西多摩・日の出町のおおばキャンプ村。都心で育まれたダンスミュージックカルチャーを東京の森へ持ち込み、多摩産材の薪割りやシルクスクリーンなど、手でつくる体験も展開する。英国VOID Acousticsのサウンドが林間に響く中、昼から翌朝まで、それぞれの過ごし方で森と音楽に触れる2日間。音楽をきっかけに、森林との新しい関係をつくる実験だ。

A camping-style all-night rave presented by Universal Music Box/UMB, a crew that has been putting on events across Tokyo's club scene. The project was launched by members who grew up close to the forest and studied forestry, built around the concept of "coexistence between forests and music." The setting is Ooba Camp Village in Hinode Town, Nishitama — an area where roughly 80% of the land is forest. Dance music culture nurtured in the city center is carried into the forests of Tokyo, alongside hands-on experiences such as splitting firewood from Tama-grown timber and silkscreen printing. As sound from the UK's VOID Acoustics resonates through the trees, the two days run from midday through to the following morning, leaving each person free to encounter the forest and the music in their own way. It's an experiment in forging a new relationship with the forest, with music as the point of entry.
