Wonderfruit's Kyoto edition transplants the Thai festival's sustainable ethos and genre-spanning programming to Japan's ancient capital. Expect forward-thinking bookings that span leftfield house, experimental electronics, and ambient soundscapes within thoughtfully designed natural settings.
