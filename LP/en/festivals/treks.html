TREKsは、京都市左京区久多の大黒谷キャンプ場で開かれる野外フェスティバル。掲げるのは「Temporary Commune of Music, Art & Nature」。杉やヒノキ、天然のブナ林に囲まれ、渓流が流れる森で、キャンプやハンモック、焚き火を楽しむ人、駆け回る子どもたち、朝まで踊る人が、それぞれの時間を過ごしながらひとつの空間を共有する。音楽は時代を超えて聴かれてきたクラシックスと、現在進行形のダンスミュージック。世界で培われた感性と日本各地のカルチャーが、この森で交差する。

TREKs is an open-air festival held at Daikokudani Campground in Kuta, Sakyo-ku, Kyoto. Its guiding banner reads "Temporary Commune of Music, Art & Nature." In a forest of cedar, cypress and native beech, threaded by a mountain stream, campers and hammock-dwellers, people gathered around the fire, children tearing about, and dancers who keep going until morning all share a single space while moving through time at their own pace. The music spans classics that have endured across eras and dance music unfolding in the present tense. Sensibilities honed around the world meet the cultures of Japan's various regions, right here in these woods.
