LOA-LOST PARADISE-

Nalu Beach — Ibaraki↗

LOA-LOST PARADISEは、太平洋に面した茨城・鉾田の nalu beach で開かれる、「New style beach party」を掲げたビーチパーティー。構成は「Sunset to Sunset」。日没から翌日の日没まで、夜の闇に脈打つ低音と、朝の光に踊るグルーヴが地続きで繋がる。ジャンルも世代も年齢も問わない、ただ踊るための場所であることを掲げる。

LOA-LOST PARADISE is a beach party on the Pacific coast at nalu beach in Hokota, Ibaraki, billed by its organisers as a new style beach party. The structure is sunset to sunset. Low end pulsing through the dark and grooves moving in the morning light run continuously into one another, across one unbroken stretch of time. The stated intention is a floor with no lines drawn across genre, generation or age. Somewhere to dance, and nothing more complicated than that.
