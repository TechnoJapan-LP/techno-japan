Transcendence

TECHNO

Transcendence delivers uncompromising techno programming in the mountainous terrain surrounding Nagano. The festival leverages Japan's natural acoustics and topography to create an immersive experience that bridges underground electronic music culture with the country's distinctive festival ecosystem. Its focus remains firmly on the music, drawing both domestic and international artists to perform in this unique alpine setting.
