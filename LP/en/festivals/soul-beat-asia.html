SOUL BEAT ASIA

Toyota Obridge, Aichi↗
OFFICIAL SITE

愛知県豊田市の豊田大橋下・千石公園にて開催される、完全 DIY 精神に基づく自給自足型の野外音楽フェスティバルです。パンクバンド TURTLE ISLAND をはじめとする有志が主宰。「伝統」「アジア」「土着」をテーマに、日本や世界各地の伝統芸能、民謡、アンダーグラウンド・ミュージックからアートまでが交差し、手作りの舞台や露店が立ち並ぶ独自のコミュニティ空間を形成します。投げ銭制（入場無料）を基本とし、投げ銭や協力金で運営される、自由で力強いエネルギーに満ちた唯一無二の草の根型カルチャー・イベントです。

Held beneath the Toyota Ohashi Bridge in Sengoku Park, Toyota City, Aichi Prefecture, this is a self-sufficient open-air music festival built entirely on a DIY ethos. It is organized by a collective of volunteers led by the punk band TURTLE ISLAND. Taking "tradition," "Asia," and "the indigenous" as its themes, the festival brings together traditional performing arts and folk music from Japan and around the world, underground music, and art, forming a singular community space lined with handmade stages and street stalls. Admission is free, with the event running on a donation basis — sustained by tips and contributions from those who attend. It is a one-of-a-kind grassroots cultural gathering, overflowing with a free and powerful energy.
