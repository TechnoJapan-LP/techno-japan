JUL 3 — 5, 2026

Moment

Dorogawa Camp Site — Nara↗

2021年に始まり、奈良・天川村の洞川キャンプ場で開催されてきた野外フェスティバル。霊峰・大峯山のふもと、1300年の祈りが息づく特別な地で、「この瞬間を謳歌する」というコンセプトを掲げ続けてきた。初開催から拡張のフェーズを経て、2026年は「深化」へと進む。音楽体験、音響、空間演出、アート、そして滞在そのものを、より繊細に、より有機的に結び直し、メインフロアのサウンドシステムはゼロから一新された。各フロアや出店エリアに加え、アート&チルスペース、ワークショップ、保育士常駐のキッズエリアなど、誰もが安心して過ごせる環境づくりにも力を注ぐ。祈りの地に身を委ね、いまこの瞬間だけに向き合う3日間だ。

An open-air festival that launched in 2021 and has since been held at Dorogawa Campground in Tenkawa Village, Nara. At the foot of the sacred Mount Ōmine, on a singular patch of land where 1,300 years of prayer still breathe, it has held fast to a single concept: to celebrate this very moment. Having passed through a phase of expansion since its first edition, 2026 marks a move toward "deepening." Musical experience, sound, spatial design, art, and the act of staying itself are being rewoven more delicately and more organically, with the main floor's sound system rebuilt from the ground up. Alongside the various floors and vendor areas, the festival is also investing in an environment where everyone can feel at ease — an art & chill space, workshops, and a kids' area staffed by professional childcare workers. Three days of surrendering to a land of prayer and facing nothing but the present moment.
