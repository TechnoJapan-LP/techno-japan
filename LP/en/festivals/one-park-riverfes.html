ONE PARK RIVERFES

HOUSETECHNO

ONE PARK RIVERFES brings electronic music to the scenic riverside setting of Gifu Prefecture. The festival combines the natural beauty of its riverbank location with carefully curated lineups spanning house and techno. Its intimate scale allows for a focused experience away from Japan's major metropolitan festival circuit.
