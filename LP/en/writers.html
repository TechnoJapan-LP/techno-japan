01 — ABOUT

Write for TECHNO JAPAN

We want to document Japan's underground dance music scene through the eyes of the people who are actually there.

We're looking for reports, interviews, guides and short news pieces.

02 — WHY WRITE

What you get

  • Your piece reaches TECHNO JAPAN's followers — people who love dance music — directly
  • It's published under your byline and collected on your writer page
  • Published in both Japanese and English; the editors prepare the translation
  • Articles link automatically to the festival, artist and venue database, so they keep being read from related pages
  • TECHNO JAPAN is indexed by Google News and found through web search
  • Editing, photo layout and search optimisation are handled by the editors — you focus on writing
  • Every piece is announced on Instagram and Threads
