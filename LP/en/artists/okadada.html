Artists/okadada

okadada

全国各地、多岐にわたるパーティーでDJとして出演し、ネットレーベル「maltine records」やbandcampで楽曲をリリース。大小、上下、場所、種別を問わず野外フェスから地下のパーティーまで出演、遊び、類例の無い幅広さの活動で各所に存在、評価を確立する。

Performing as a DJ at a wide range of parties across Japan, with releases on the net label maltine records and on Bandcamp. Appearing at—and simply hanging out at—everything from outdoor festivals to underground parties, regardless of scale, status, location or genre, he has carved out a presence and earned recognition across the board through an unparalleled breadth of activity.
