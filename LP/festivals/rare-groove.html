Rare Groove

HOUSETECHNOMINIMAL

Rare Groove brings together Japan's underground electronic music community in the natural surroundings of Yamanashi Prefecture. The festival focuses on deeper cuts and alternative sounds, drawing selectors who prioritize musical discovery over mainstream appeal. Its intimate scale and curated programming have established it as a destination for those seeking authentic dance music experiences away from urban club circuits.
